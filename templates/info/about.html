Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How does the AI Fertilizer Recommendation Engine calculate the dosage?
The algorithm assesses existing soil test values (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, pH) against scientifically calibrated crop target curves. It then factors in soil percolation/leaching indices and climate parameters (temperature, humidity, rainfall) to compute the exact deficit, selecting the most cost-effective and bio-available fertilizer formulation.
Why is soil pH critical for fertilizer efficiency?
Soil pH dictates the bioavailability of nutrients. In highly acidic soils (pH < 5.5), phosphorus binds with aluminum and iron, becoming unavailable to plants. In alkaline soils (pH > 8.0), micronutrients like Zinc, Iron, and Manganese precipitate out. Our system recommends lime or gypsum corrections to restore optimal uptake.
How accurate is the Sales and Demand Forecasting module?
Our forecasting engine uses Scikit-Learn linear regression supplemented with cyclical crop calendar seasonality factors. On historical agronomic datasets, it achieves an R² score exceeding 85% with Low Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), assisting dealers and cooperatives in preventing stockouts.
What is Sentiment Analysis in the context of agriculture?
Sentiment analysis uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to classify market reports, weather warnings, and government subsidy declarations into Positive, Neutral, or Negative indicators. This empowers farmers to make proactive pricing and sowing decisions.
